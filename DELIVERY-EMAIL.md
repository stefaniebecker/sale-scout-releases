## ⏱️ Do this first — it takes two minutes

Before you install anything, check you can get Amazon API access. It's the one
part we don't control, and it's much better to know now.

It's well hidden, so here's the exact route:

**1.** Go to **amazon.com**, signed in as the main account holder (not a team
member's login — Amazon only lets the primary owner do this).

**2.** **Top left corner** — click **SiteStripe**, the bar that says
*Influencers and Associates*.

**3.** That opens your Associates home page.

**4.** **Top right corner** — click the **hamburger menu** (three stacked lines).

**5.** Click **Tools**, then **Creators API**.

**6.** See if you can register.

**Three things Amazon requires:**

• Your Associates account has been fully accepted (not still provisional)
• You're signed in as the primary account owner
• **At least 10 qualifying sales in the last 30 days**

If you can register — brilliant, do it now. Approval takes a few days and
starting now means the clock runs while you set everything else up.

If you can't, email me at hello@stefaniebeckerdigital.com and tell me what it
said. Depending on the reason, it may just be a form away, or it may mean
waiting until your sales catch up. Either way I'd rather sort it with you than
have you stuck.

## Two things so nothing feels broken

**The first setup involves some waiting, and that's normal.** Claude installs a
few things your Mac needs. If your Mac has never been used for this kind of
thing, you may see an Apple pop-up asking to install "command line developer
tools" — click **Install** and let it run.

Between that and a browser component Claude downloads, expect **20 to 40
minutes** for the first setup, most of it just waiting while things download.
Your actual hands-on time is about 15 minutes. You can walk away and come back.

**Amazon is the slow part, and it's first.** Sale Scout needs Creators API
access from your Amazon Associates account. It's free, but Amazon reviews it
and requires **at least 10 qualifying sales in the past 30 days**. Claude starts
you on that application first so the clock is running, then sets up everything
else while you wait.

If you don't have 10 sales in the last month yet, you can't get access from
Amazon yet — that's their rule, not ours. Everything else will be ready and
waiting for the day you do.
