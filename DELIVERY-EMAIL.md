# Delivery email / Stan thank-you message

Plain text. Paste straight into Stan.

Deliberately short. The Amazon steps are *not* in here — Claude walks them
through it one click at a time, which works far better than a list in an email
that looks like homework. All this email has to do is get them to open Claude
and say one sentence.

Subject: Your Sale Scout is here — two sentences to get started

---

Hi {customer_name}!

Thank you! Sale Scout is below. 🎉

{product_files}

HERE'S ALL YOU DO

1. Download and unzip the file.

2. Open the Claude Code app. Not Claude in your browser — the desktop app, from
claude.ai/download. You'll need a paid Claude plan.

3. Paste this to Claude, exactly:

"Please install the Sale Scout skill. The file is sale-scout.zip in my Downloads
folder."

4. Then paste this:

"Set up Sale Scout"

That's it. Claude takes it from there and walks you through every step, one at a
time. You won't type a single command, and you can ask it anything along the
way — including "I don't understand what you just said." It's very good at that.

TWO THINGS TO EXPECT 👀

Amazon is the slow part, and Claude starts you there. Sale Scout needs free
Creators API access from your Amazon Associates account. Amazon reviews it,
which takes a few days, so Claude gets that moving first and sets everything
else up while you wait.

Heads up: Amazon requires at least 10 qualifying sales in the last 30 days. If
you're not there yet, that's their rule and not ours — set Sale Scout up anyway
and it'll be ready and waiting for the day you qualify.

The first setup goes quiet for a few minutes. Claude installs a piece behind the
scenes and it looks like nothing is happening. It's working. Let it finish.

WHEN YOU'RE RUNNING

One email every morning: what's on sale in your storefront, how much it dropped,
and links ready to paste.

Ask Claude anything in plain English — "only show me 30% off or more", "stop
showing me that cooler", "run it now". You're the boss!

IF YOU GET STUCK 🛠️

Say this to Claude:

"Run the Sale Scout check-up"

It tests every piece and tells you in plain language which one needs fixing.
