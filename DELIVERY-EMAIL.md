## Two things so nothing feels broken

**The first setup is quiet for a few minutes.** Claude installs a browser
component behind the scenes and it looks like nothing is happening. It's
working. Let it finish.

**Amazon is the slow part, and it's first.** Sale Scout needs Creators API
access from your Amazon Associates account. It's free, but Amazon reviews it
and requires **at least 10 qualifying sales in the past 30 days**. Claude starts
you on that application first so the clock is running, then sets up everything
else while you wait.

If you don't have 10 sales in the last month yet, you can't get access from
Amazon yet — that's their rule, not ours. Everything else will be ready and
waiting for the day you do.
