# Delivery email / Stan thank-you message

Plain text. Paste straight into Stan.

No Amazon registration steps — Claude walks them through that one click at a
time, which works far better than a list in an email that reads like homework.

The email's only job is to get them to open Claude and say one sentence.

What's deliberately kept: the timing expectation and the Apple pop-up, because
people forgive a slow setup but not a surprise one; and one line about Amazon
being slow, so nobody wonders why they aren't getting emails on day one.

Subject: Your Sale Scout is here — four steps

---

Hi {customer_name}!

Thank you! Sale Scout is attached — and your download link is below. 🎉

{product_files}

HERE'S ALL YOU DO

Step 1 — Download and unzip the file. You'll get a folder called sale-scout.

Step 2 — Open the Claude Code app. Not Claude in your browser — the desktop
app, from claude.ai/download. You'll need a paid Claude plan.

Step 3 — Copy and paste this to Claude, exactly:

"Please install the Sale Scout skill. The file is sale-scout.zip in my Downloads
folder."

Step 4 — Then paste this:

"Set up Sale Scout"

That's it. You won't type a single command. Claude takes over and walks you
through every step, one at a time — and you can ask it anything along the way,
including "I don't understand what you just said." It's very good at that.

SO NOTHING FEELS BROKEN 👀

The first setup involves waiting, and that's normal. Claude installs a few
things your Mac needs. If your Mac has never been used for this kind of thing,
you may see an Apple pop-up asking to install "command line developer tools" —
click Install and let it run.

All in, expect 20 to 40 minutes, most of it just waiting while things download.
Your hands-on time is about 15 minutes. You can walk away and come back.

There are also a few quiet stretches where it looks like nothing is happening.
It's working. Let it finish.

One part is slower than the rest: Amazon has to approve your access before Sale
Scout can see prices, and that takes a few days. Claude starts you there first
so the clock is running, then sets up everything else while you wait. So don't
worry if your first email doesn't arrive tomorrow.

WHEN YOU'RE RUNNING

You'll get one email every morning: what's on sale in your storefront, how much
it dropped, and links ready to paste.

Ask Claude anything in plain English — "only show me 30% off or more", "stop
showing me that cooler", "run it now", "send me a test email". You're the boss!

IF YOU GET STUCK 🛠️

One shortcut solves most things. Say this to Claude:

"Run the Sale Scout check-up"

It tests every piece separately and tells you in plain language which one needs
fixing.

And if that doesn't do it, email hello@stefaniebeckerdigital.com. You have 30
days of support, and we'd genuinely rather hear from you than have you give up
on it.

Thank you for being one of the first.
